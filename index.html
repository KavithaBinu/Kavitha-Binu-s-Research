About My Work

I am a dedicated historian focusing on the religious and cultural transformations in southern India. My academic journey is driven by uncovering localized historical narratives and presenting them through rigorous archival research and regional studies.

Current Thesis

Redemption of Jainism in Kanyakumari District

This doctoral research explores the historical presence, decline, and subsequent revival phases of Jainism in the Kanyakumari region. By examining epigraphical, archaeological, and literary sources, the thesis provides a comprehensive analysis of Jainism's socio-cultural footprint in Tamil Nadu.
